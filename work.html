About The fest

Black Clover features several exciting events and battles that showcase the strength, courage, and determination of its characters. Major events include the Magic Knights Entrance Exam, where young mages compete to join the Magic Knights; the Royal Knights Selection Exam, where powerful fighters compete for selection; the Star Awards Festival, which celebrates the achievements of Magic Knights squads; and the Spade Kingdom Raid, where Asta and his friends fight against powerful enemies. These events highlight teamwork, friendship, rivalry, and the journey to become the Wizard King.
